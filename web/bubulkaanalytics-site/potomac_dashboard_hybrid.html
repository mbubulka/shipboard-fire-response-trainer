Research Methodology

Forecasting Model

Method: Linear Trend Regression with Seasonal Adjustment

The 7-day flow forecast uses ordinary least squares (OLS) linear regression on the most recent 7 days of discharge data. The model calculates the daily trend rate and projects forward to generate predictions. Seasonal adjustment factors are applied based on historical monthly patterns.

Forecast Accuracy: ±15% typical error at 7-day horizon

Data Sources & Safety Scoring

Primary Data Source: USGS Water Services API (waterdata.usgs.gov)

Station: 01646500 - Potomac River at Little Falls Lock, MD

Update Frequency: Real-time data every 15 minutes

Safety Algorithm: Multi-factor assessment combining flow rate, trend stability, seasonal patterns, and paddler experience level
