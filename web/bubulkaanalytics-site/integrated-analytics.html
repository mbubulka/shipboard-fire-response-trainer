🎯 The Challenge We're Solving

🎖️ Military Family Dilemma

Service members and their families face complex education decisions:

  • Multiple GI Bill benefit options with different coverage rates
  • 15,000+ colleges with varying costs and outcomes
  • Geographic constraints due to military assignments
  • Need to maximize limited education benefits
  • Complex cost-benefit calculations for family planning

📊 Data Complexity Problem

Federal education data presents massive analytical challenges:

  • 6,163+ institutions across all 50 states
  • 22,000+ cost scenarios (in-state, out-state, programs)
  • Multiple funding sources and benefit calculations
  • Geographic cost variations requiring spatial analysis
  • ROI modeling across different family situations
